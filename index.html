How it works

A sleep schedule app that meets you where you are

Three steps, set up in two minutes. Bjørn the bear does the maths and the reminding.

1

Set your wake times

Pick a wake time for each weekday and how much sleep you need. BedBear works out tonight's bedtime: wake time minus sleep need minus 15 minutes to fall asleep.

2

Wind down in 3 small steps

Dim the lights 47 minutes before bed, put your phone on the charger at 30, and read a few pages at 15. A wind down routine that's small enough to actually do.

3

Hold to go to bed

When it's time, hold the button for two seconds to say you're going to bed. Each on-time night grows your streak, and you can share it when you're proud.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is revenge bedtime procrastination?

It's staying up later than you meant to, with no outside reason, often to win back some time for yourself after a full day. Researchers call it bedtime procrastination. Scrolling, one more episode, racing thoughts and simply losing track of time are the usual reasons.

How does BedBear work out my bedtime?

You set your wake time for each weekday and how much sleep you need. BedBear subtracts your sleep need and 15 minutes to fall asleep. Up at 6:30 AM with 7h 30m of sleep need gives a bedtime of 10:45 PM.

What happens if I miss my bedtime?

Nothing is ruined. BedBear never locks you out or shames you. It shows your next best time and how much sleep you still get. One late night costs only a little, and your streak holds if you're back on time the next night.

Does BedBear block apps on my phone?

No. Hard limits and app blockers are easy to switch off, and they often feel like punishment. BedBear uses a short wind down routine and timely, gentle nudges instead.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. On iPhone, BedBear shows a Live Activity on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island on supported models. On Android, the same wind down appears as an ongoing notification card.

Is BedBear free?

BedBear is free to download. BedBear Pro is an optional monthly or yearly subscription, and the yearly plan starts with a 7-day free trial. Payment is handled by the App Store or Google Play, and you can cancel any time in your store settings. See our terms.

Is BedBear a medical or sleep tracking app?

No. BedBear helps you build a habit of going to bed on time. It does not diagnose or treat sleep disorders and is not medical advice. If you have ongoing trouble sleeping, talk to a doctor.

Which languages does BedBear support?

BedBear is available in 15 languages, with English as the default.
