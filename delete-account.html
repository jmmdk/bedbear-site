Delete your BedBear account

Last updated: 12 November 2026

You can delete your BedBear account (listed in the stores as "Go To Bed: BedBear") and the data linked to it at any time. BedBear is made by Useful Apps ApS. This page explains how, what is deleted, and what is kept.

1. Delete it in the app

  1. Open BedBear and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away and the app starts over from the beginning.

If you use BedBear without signing in, the same button deletes the plan and sleep logs stored on your phone.

2. What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details (email address, Sign in with Apple or Google link).
  • Your sleep plan, onboarding answers, sleep type and preferences.
  • Your nightly logs, wind-down steps and "what did you do instead" answers.
  • Push notification and Live Activity tokens, and the pre-rendered Live Activity texts.
  • Your analytics profile (person) and the events linked to it.
  • Your subscription status record with our subscription provider.

Data is removed from our live systems when you delete your account, and from backups within a further 30 days, as described in our Privacy Policy.

3. What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchase records at Apple and Google: Apple and Google handle billing and keep their own records of your purchases under their own privacy policies. We cannot delete those.
  • Our own purchase records: where we have to keep records of a transaction, we keep them as long as Danish bookkeeping law requires (currently 5 years from the end of the financial year).
  • Support emails: if you wrote to us, the conversation is kept for up to 24 months after it ends.

4. Cancel your subscription separately

Deleting your account does not cancel BedBear Pro. Apple and Google handle billing, so please cancel the subscription in the store, ideally before you delete your account:

  • iPhone: App Store subscriptions, or Settings > your name > Subscriptions.
  • Android: Google Play subscriptions, or Google Play > profile > Payments & subscriptions.

5. No access to the app? Email us

You can also ask us to delete your account by email. Write from the email address linked to your account, with the subject "Delete my account", so we can confirm it is yours. We handle the request within 30 days and reply when it is done.

Email hi@bedbear.app

If you signed in with Apple and chose to hide your email, write from any address and tell us roughly when you signed up; we may ask a follow-up question to confirm the account is yours.

6. Questions

Email hi@bedbear.app. Read more about how we handle data in our Privacy Policy.
